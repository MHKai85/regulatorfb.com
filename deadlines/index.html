FSIS swine inspection effective date, July 20, 2026

FSIS is amending its regulations to end mandatory mandibular lymph node incision and viscera palpation of swine carcasses in all swine slaughter establishments, including establishments operating under traditional swine slaughter inspection or the New Swine Slaughter Inspection System.

Primary source: Visual Post-Mortem Inspection in Swine Slaughter Establishments
