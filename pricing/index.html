FAQ

Is the first brief really free?

Yes. The first custom brief is free so you can judge whether the filtering is useful for your operation.

No. Regulator provides regulatory monitoring and operational triage support. It is informational, not legal or regulatory advice.

No. The current path is email-first. The intake profile is used to prepare the first brief and follow up.

Can the profile change?

Yes. If your products, states, packaging, ingredients, or priorities change, the monitoring profile should change with them.
