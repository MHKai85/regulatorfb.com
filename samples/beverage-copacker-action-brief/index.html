Sample Weekly Action Brief for a Beverage Co-Packer

This sample shows the shape of a Regulator Weekly Action Brief: action-required items, monitor-only items, no-action filtering, and source appendix expectations for a beverage co-packer profile.

Download sample PDF

Sample profile

U.S. beverage co-packer serving CPG customers across functional beverages, juices, RTD alcohol, carbonated soft drinks, sparkling water, and adjacent beverage categories.

Action-required examples

  • Functional beverage claims and labeling review window.
  • State packaging and container compliance check for multi-state beverage customers.
  • Aluminum can, packaging input, tariff, and supply-chain review.

Monitor examples

  • TTB, RTD cocktail, and hard seltzer label or formulation watch.
  • Juice and beverage pathogen or recall watch.
  • Ingredient, additive, color, and flavor rulemaking watch.
  • Citric acid and imported beverage ingredient input watch.

No-action filtering examples

  • USDA meat and poultry notices for a beverage-only profile.
  • Broad labor or OSHA penalty updates outside the Regulator watchlist scope.
  • International-only standards with no U.S. customer or import exposure.
